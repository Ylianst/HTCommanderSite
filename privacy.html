Privacy policy

In short: we do not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Overview

HT Commander ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. In short: we do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.

Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal or user data. Specifically, the App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Device identifiers or usage analytics
  • Crash or diagnostic reports
  • Any other personally identifiable information

Permissions

The App may request certain device permissions (such as Bluetooth, microphone, or location) solely to provide its core functionality — for example, connecting to and communicating with a compatible radio. Any data accessed through these permissions stays on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any data.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this file with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project's repository.
