Audio & voice

Bluetooth Audio

Transmit and receive through your computer's speakers, microphone or headset over a Bluetooth audio link — with live visualization and full device controls.

← All features

Two-way audio, no cables

HTCommander supports two-way Bluetooth audio. You can transmit and receive using your computer's audio devices and/or a headset, and record radio audio to a WAV file — all without extra cabling.

Dial in your whole audio chain

An audio visualization plus plenty of device-selection and volume controls let you tune everything to sound just right: pick input and output devices and set your levels. To transmit, simply press and hold the transmit button.

Available on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.
