Three steps

How to get started

You'll need a supported handheld radio, a device with Bluetooth, and an amateur radio license to transmit.

1

Get a radio

Pick up any supported Benshi handheld, such as a BTech UV-Pro or Vero VR-N76, and charge it up.

2

Install the app

Download HTCommander for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android or iOS — or open the web version in Chrome or Edge.

3

Pair & operate

Pair the radio over Bluetooth, load your channels and start messaging, mapping and moving data.

Yours, entirely

No accounts. No tracking. No servers.

HTCommander does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Permissions like Bluetooth and microphone are used only to talk to your radio — everything stays on your device.

  • ✓ No analytics, ads or third-party trackers
  • ✓ No sign-up and no cloud account required
  • ✓ Free & open source under the Apache 2.0 license
