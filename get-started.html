Get started

On the air in three steps

You'll need a supported handheld radio, a device with Bluetooth, and an amateur radio license to transmit. Get information on a license here.

1

Get a radio

Pick up any supported Benshi handheld — such as a BTech UV-Pro or Vero VR-N76 — and make sure its Bluetooth is enabled.

2

Install the app

Download the build for your platform below, or open the web version in Chrome or Edge.

3

Pair & operate

Pair the radio over Bluetooth, load your channels, and start messaging, mapping and moving data.

Step 1

Supported radios

HTCommander works with these Bluetooth-capable handheld radios.

Step 3

Pair over Bluetooth

Turn on your radio's Bluetooth, open HTCommander and select your radio when it appears. Once paired, HTCommander reads your channels and unlocks messaging, mapping, audio and data features for your platform.

  • ✓ Works over standard Bluetooth — no extra cables or interfaces
  • ✓ Audio, speech and SSTV on desktop and Android
  • ✓ An amateur radio license is required to transmit
