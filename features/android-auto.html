In the car

Android Auto

Access HTCommander channels, connected radios and APRS messages from your vehicle's Android Auto display.

← All features

Your radio controls on the road

HTCommander's Android Auto integration brings the parts of the app you need in the car to the vehicle display. Browse your channel list and select the channel you want without leaving the Android Auto interface.

Radios and messages at a glance

See available HTCommander radios and choose which one to use. The messages view keeps incoming APRS traffic visible in the same familiar Android Auto experience.

Available on Android devices that support Android Auto.
